How to read the result

The 90% interval

Two additional models predict the 5th and 95th percentile of charges for each input. The space between them is a per-applicant range that needs no ground truth to compute.

The low-confidence flag

Applicants whose interval is wider than the 90th-percentile width seen on the test set are flagged. Those are the cases where automated pricing is least reliable.
